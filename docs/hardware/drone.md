# Drone (Frame & Flight Controller)

The drone is given fully assembled. Here are information about the drone frame and the flight controller.

## SpeedyBee BEE35 Pro 3.5 CineWhoop Frame Kit

The [SpeedyBee BEE35 Pro 3.5 CineWhoop Frame Kit](https://www.speedybee.com/speedybee-bee35-3-5-inch-frame/#Parameters) is a 3.5-inch cinewhoop frame specially designed for the DJI O3 Air Unit. It has dedicated heat-dissipation hardware, convenient access to the Type-C port and memory card, and support for other compatible 20×20 video transmitters. It also support external cameras such as GoPro and compact RunCam models for higher-quality cinematic capture. With these features, this frame becomes an ideal solution for cinematic cruising, close-proximity flying, and action-camera footage.

## Flywoo GOKU GN745 45A AIO

The [Flywoo GOKU GN745 45A AIO 2–6S AM32](https://flywoo.net/products/goku-gn745-45a-aio-bl_32-mpu6000-v3) is a high-performance all-in-one flight controller and 4-in-1 ESC board for compact FPV drones. By combining the FC and ESCs onto a single 33.5 × 33.5 mm board, it reduces wiring, weight, and the amount of space needed inside the frame.

At its core is an STM32F745 32-bit processor running at 216 MHz, paired with an onboard gyro, barometer, 16 MB Blackbox storage, and seven hardware UARTs. The integrated ESC supports 2S–6S LiPo batteries, delivers 45A continuous current, and runs AM32 firmware, supporting protocols up to DShot1200.
